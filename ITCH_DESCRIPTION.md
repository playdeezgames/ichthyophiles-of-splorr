## Page text

**Latest version:** https://playdeezgames.github.io/ichthyophiles-of-splorr/ (also add it as an external link on the page)

**Ichthyophiles of SPLORR!!** is a metaphor about keeping a single fish alive.

You start with no fish. Get a fish. Feed the fish. Not too much: the fish dies if you overfeed it. Not too little: the fish dies if you starve it.

You can feed it as often as you like. You never get to see how much food is in the tank, only how the fish looks: plump and glossy, lean and keeping an eye on you, or something worse.

Yer fish lives on the real clock. It eats while you are at work. It gets hungry while you sleep. It does not care that you closed the tab.

The metaphor keeps count: how many fish you overfed, how many you starved, and the longest any fish of yours has lived.

### How to play

- Buttons only: Get a Fish, Feed, Rename, How to Play, Reset.
- Each fish gets a name. You can give it a better one.
- Come back later. Then come back again.
- Yer fish and yer record save in the browser. Clearing site data, or a different browser or device, means a different tank.
- Found a bug that is not a metaphor? Leave a comment (or use "Send feedback" under the metaphor, when it is shown).

### About it

Ichthyophiles of SPLORR!! grew out of "Feed the Fish", game 02 of TheGrumpyGameDev's Fifty Games project (a VB.NET console version). This one is rebuilt from the ground up for the browser in Odin compiled to WebAssembly.
